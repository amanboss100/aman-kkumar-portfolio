About Me

Learning by understanding and building.

Hi, I'm Aman. I'm an Electronics Engineering student interested in how hardware, embedded systems and AI can work together.

I got interested in electronics because I wanted to understand what happens behind the things we use every day — especially circuits, microcontrollers and machines.

What I like about embedded systems is that you can write some code and actually see it do something in the real world. That's what made me interested in boards like the ESP32.

I'm also learning about AI because I don't want to only use AI tools. I want to understand the technology behind them and eventually connect AI with real hardware.

Right now, I'm starting with the basics — circuits, LEDs, breadboards and electronic components — and gradually moving towards embedded systems and AI.

My goal is simple: learn, build, improve, and eventually become an Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineer.
