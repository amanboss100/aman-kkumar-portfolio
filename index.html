Who I Am

I'm an Electronics Engineering student interested in how hardware, embedded systems, and AI work together. I believe the future is built at the intersection of these three fields.

What Drives Me

I got interested in electronics because I wanted to understand what happens behind everyday devices — especially circuits, microcontrollers, and machines. What excites me about embedded systems is that you write code and actually see it control something in the real world.

Current Focus

Right now, I'm building my foundation in circuits, breadboards, and electronic components. I'm also experimenting with the ESP32 and learning how software can control hardware. I'm exploring AI not just as a tool, but also understanding the technology behind it.

Long-term Goal

Become an Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineer who can bridge hardware and intelligent systems. I'm committed to learning deeply, building continuously, and improving with every project.
